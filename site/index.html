HOW IT WORKS

Separate by design.
Connected by choice.

Tarium is an index and shared framework, not a monorepo. Member projects retain their identity, data, and release cycle.

01

Independent repositories

Every project lives in its own public repository with its own development and documentation.

02

Knowledge made explicit

Projects maintain their own documented ontologies and navigable galleries.

03

A deliberate public boundary

Membership is explicit, sources are reviewed, and public work is independent of nonpublic resources.
