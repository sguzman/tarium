# PROJECT_NAME Constitution

This is a template for an independent **public** repository. Fill in the project identity and keep the mandatory articles intact.

## 1. Public-information boundary (mandatory)

No private chats, prompts, conversation excerpts, personal notes, private venting or internal tone, private identifiers, unpublished research, secrets, or materials of uncertain provenance may be included in code, documentation, comments, examples, galleries, issues, commits, releases, logs, or repository history. Paraphrased disclosures count. Write public technical material in a neutral, relevant voice.

Inventory and exclude private material before extracting anything from a nonpublic source. Never import a private repository's Git history. If publication status is uncertain, exclude by default. Automated leak checks supplement but cannot replace content and provenance review.

## 2. Independent, public operation (mandatory)

The repository owns the complete public project: code, necessary build/runtime assets or openly documented public inputs, documentation, ontology, and gallery. Its primary function may not depend on unpublished repositories, private services, private ontologies, or private workspace paths.

## 3. Ontology and gallery (mandatory)

Maintain a full project-specific ontology in human-readable and machine-readable formats at `ontology/`. Maintain a public, navigable gallery and machine-readable gallery index at `gallery/`. A new gallery may be explicitly empty rather than populated with fabricated examples.

## 4. Public integration boundary (mandatory)

Refer to another Tarium project only if it is a publicly available, explicitly registered Tarium project. No private Taria project or other unpublished project may be referenced or required as an integration, build input, schema dependency, or operational dependency. Other genuinely public third-party dependencies may be used with proper attribution and documentation.

## 5. Membership and governance (mandatory)

Only the Tarium repository owner may explicitly authorize this project's registry membership. Reusing this charter, naming conventions, or subject area does not enroll it. Project-specific rules may strengthen but never relax mandatory Tarium protections.

## 6. Project-specific provisions

Add domain-specific technical, licensing, accessibility, data quality, and contribution rules here.
